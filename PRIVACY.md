# Privacy and support

The current Mongrel Dictionary evaluation app searches its bundled dictionaries on your Mac. It has no account service, advertising integration, or app analytics. The app does not send your lookup terms to a Mongrel server.

Recent lookups, saved words, the last query, lookup counts, and appearance preferences are stored locally in the app's macOS storage. They are not synchronized between Macs by Dictionary. Your own backup tools may include this local data.

**Clear Desk also clears lookup history.** It is not a complete reset of saved words, preferences, or every stored lookup statistic. Do not use it as a promise that all app data has been erased.

macOS may perform its own download, signature, security, and diagnostic checks according to your system settings. GitHub receives normal website and download requests when you visit this product page or download a release. Those services have their own privacy practices; the app's local lookup design does not describe their behavior.

## Reporting a problem

Include the app build, macOS version, Mac model, lookup mode, and concise reproduction steps. Share an example term only if you are comfortable making it public.

Before attaching a screenshot or crash report, review it for names, account details, document titles, local paths, and lookup history. Never post passwords, signing credentials, private dictionaries, recovery backups, or a whole home-folder archive. A small example usually gives a clearer report.

Accessibility reports are welcome. Describe the control, keyboard sequence or assistive technology, what you expected, and what happened. A statement that something is difficult to use is useful feedback even without a proposed fix.
